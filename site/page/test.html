A select in a field

The first option is <option value="" selected disabled hidden> and stands in for the placeholder: while it is the one chosen and the select is closed, the label lies over the select; with focus, or with another option chosen, it is small at the top.

A select with several options

With multiple the options are a list, always open: the label stays small at the top. Chosen options take the primary color, a disabled one a lighter one.
