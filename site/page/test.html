Modal — dialogAnimationLeft / Right / Top / Bottom

Each panel slides in from its edge of the screen and goes back there on close (X, backdrop click, Esc).

Left

Slides in from the left edge, docked on the left.

Right

Slides in from the right edge, docked on the right.

Top

Comes down from the top edge to the center.

Bottom

Comes up from the bottom edge to the center.
