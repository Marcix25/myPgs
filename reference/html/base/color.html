Text on a color

Each --color-*-text is the reading colour that belongs to its own background, so a coloured surface gets legible text without anyone picking a contrast by hand. Retheme the background and the pair follows.

background['bgPrimary'] + textColor['txtPrimaryText']
background['bgSecondary'] + textColor['txtSecondaryText']
background['bgTertiary'] + textColor['txtTertiaryText']
background['bgQuaternary'] + textColor['txtQuaternaryText']
